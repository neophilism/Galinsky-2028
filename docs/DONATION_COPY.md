# Campaign-supplied donation copy

Source: the campaign owner's website brief. Preserve verbatim in the home-page donation area and dedicated donation flow. Editing these statements requires campaign/treasurer approval. The sitewide paid-for disclaimer, accurate contributor-information notice and applicable ruleset are additional requirements, not replacements for this text.

You may donate if you:

- Are a U.S. citizen or lawfully admitted permanent resident (i.e., green card holder).
- Contribute from your own funds. You cannot contribute funds that are being provided by another person or entity.
- Are at least 18 years old.
- Are not a federal contractor.
- Are making this contribution with your personal credit card and not with a corporate or business credit card or a card issued to another person.

Phillip Galinsky for America does not accept contributions from PACs, corporations, unions, federal contractors, national banks, FARA registrants, or foreign nationals.

The Phillip Galinsky For America Principal Campaign Committee is the official organization responsible for managing all aspects of Phillip Galinsky’s campaign for President of the United States.

This committee handles fundraising, spending, and compliance with Federal Election Commission (FEC) regulations. As the central hub of the campaign’s activities, the Phillip Galinsky For America Principal Campaign Committee ensures that all financial contributions and expenditures are transparent, properly reported, and in full compliance with all US federal election laws.

Contributions to Phillip Galinsky for America are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes.

## Presentation and approval controls

Preserve the wording as supplied; this file does not independently verify the committee's registered name, status or current compliance. The treasurer must confirm those factual representations before fundraising opens. Distinguish campaign choices from general legal prohibitions. The age-18 donor policy does not automatically establish a volunteer age policy.

Use approximately 14-16px text with adequate contrast and line height, beside the desktop form and directly below it on mobile. This is a design target, not a legal safe harbor. Do not hide required language in a closed disclosure, use pale low-contrast text, require scrolling inside a tiny legal-text box, or crop the copy in an iframe.

A single source/version supplies all locations. An exact-content test covers all five bullets and all four following paragraphs. The processor's own hosted path must also carry the appropriate approved rules, not bypass them.

## Owner-authorized checkout simplification — 2026-10-13

The owner expressly directed removal of the five separately presented Stripe donor-certification checkboxes and the primary/general election dropdown, replacing them with **one required checkbox** linking to the full terms. This changes the active, password-protected **Stripe Checkout entry form**, not the authoritative campaign eligibility rules above. The full terms are available at `/donate/terms` and are the single reference source for those certifications, with the FEC paid-for, nondeductibility and best-efforts notification still visible at the point of solicitation. For the independent 2028 campaign, this checkout expressly designates donations to the **general election** in the linked terms. The server records `general` without asking each donor for a dropdown selection, and securely stores terms version and agreement time.

This is not permission to omit donor eligibility checks, retroactively redesignate contributions, ignore aggregate per-election contribution limits, or use general-election funds inconsistently with FEC rules. Independent candidates may have a primary-equivalent limit despite no literal primary contest (FEC candidate contribution limits). Supporting a separate primary-equivalent fundraising channel would require treasurer approval, distinct solicitation wording and accounting; it is not included in this simplified flow.
