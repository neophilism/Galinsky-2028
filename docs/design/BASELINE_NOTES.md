# Approved baseline

The public template bodies, styles and candidate portrait come from the approved 10 October 2026 mockups. Original public HTML hashes are in BASELINE.json. The top-of-issue video is an explicit amendment. Private materials.js, source ZIPs, source Markdown and demonstration account bypasses are deliberately excluded from the public build. The full 60-package plan is preserved in docs/DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md.

The source prototype is a review artifact, not a source of account, payment or certification truth. Production replaces its preview dialogs and faux registration flow. No fonts are redistributed.
