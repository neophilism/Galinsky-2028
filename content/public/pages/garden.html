WHY IT MATTERS

A people can lose more than property.

The Garden starts with a loss that a check alone cannot describe: the destruction of nationhood, political belonging, self-government, and the ability to pass a future from one generation to the next.

The proposal would support the voluntary re-founding of republican nations connected to historic nations harmed by the slave trade. American descendants of slavery provide the founding basis; citizenship is political, not a permanent racial classification imposed by government.

THE MECHANISM

What would change.

01

Establish the historical foundation.

Use documented history to identify the nations and injuries at the center of the remedy, with honest limits on what the evidence can establish.

02

Put self-government first.

Participants choose voluntarily, write constitutions, and elect their own leaders. Government does not sort people into nations by race.

03

Negotiate a productive inheritance.

The nations negotiate a remedy built around new economic opportunities, with voluntary state participation and protection for existing lawful livelihoods.

QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

Details matter.

Is this a program people can enroll in today?

No. The Garden is a proposal requiring legislation, public participation, and legal safeguards. This campaign site does not accept ancestry records or applications for benefits.

Who decides the remedy?

The participating nations negotiate it. The proposal should not be presented as government assigning a uniform remedy or dictating people’s political identity.

Does repair require taking someone else’s livelihood?

The non-displacement safeguard is central: the remedy is designed around new opportunity, not taking an existing lawful livelihood from an innocent person.
