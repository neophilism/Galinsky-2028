GREEN / ACT WITHIN THE RULES

Start with a conversation.

Share official public links, learn an issue, or talk with people you know. Follow the Universal Core; do not collect private data or spend campaign money.

YELLOW / USE THE CAMPAIGN SYSTEM

Get the right tools.

Canvassing, phone contact, tabling, and organized outreach use the current campaign scripts, permissions, training, and opt-out process.

RED / WAIT FOR ACTIVATION

Check before you begin.

Petitions, election work, paid or mass communications, contracts, and private-data access need specific, dated authorization.
