THE PRINCIPLE BEHIND THIS CAMPAIGN

A promise is only
as strong as
our will to keep it.

Justice is not a favor from government.
It is what government owes the people.

When government takes away a person’s freedom, destroys a livelihood, or leaves a people’s losses unrepaired, the harm does not disappear with time. It becomes a debt of injustice.

This campaign starts with a simple responsibility: protect everyone’s rights, identify the people actually harmed, and repair what can be repaired — without creating a new injustice for someone else.

Not inherited guilt. Not partisan payback.
A public responsibility to put actual wrongs right.
