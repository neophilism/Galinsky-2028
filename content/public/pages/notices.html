Campaign notices.

How this website works, what is ready, and how your information is handled.

Privacy

The public website does not contain advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or a volunteer directory. When enabled, anonymous page-view analytics count time while a page is focused and visible. Temporary per-page tokens prevent double counting; metrics are deleted after 30 days. We do not store IP addresses, identifiers across pages, query strings, referrers or browsing journeys in analytics. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control browser signals are respected. Hosting providers necessarily process connection information such as IP addresses. Videos connect to YouTube only after you choose to play them.

Volunteer account access is separate from optional campaign updates. Private volunteer materials are not bundled into this public website. Sensitive submissions and live donations remain unavailable until their security and operating requirements are met.

No form on this public website creates an account, stores donor details, or processes a payment. Account features are served by the separate volunteer application when configured.

Accessibility

Use the skip link and keyboard navigation, pause the homepage animation, or use your device's reduced-motion preference. Issue summaries are readable without watching a video. Captions and transcripts will accompany approved videos.

Accessibility review is ongoing. The current build does not claim independent certification or that testing has found every barrier.

Volunteer participation

Access to materials does not authorize restricted work. Petition collection, election activities, official accounts and similar work require the applicable current training, approvals and jurisdiction instructions. Do not collect personal information in an unapproved form or improvise a campaign outreach system.

Contact

The campaign's verified support address will be published here when configured. Please do not send sensitive documents through public comments or ordinary unapproved email.

Policy publications

The extended issue summaries are available now. The approved Kirk–Evers bill and supporting documents, and the verified Garden and Cornerstone website links, will appear on their issue pages when publication is ready.
