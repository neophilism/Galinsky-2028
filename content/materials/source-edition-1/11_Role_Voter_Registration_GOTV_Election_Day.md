# Voter Registration, GOTV & Election-Day Playbook

*Help eligible voters use official systems without giving legal advice or interfering with voting.*

Direct people to authoritative registration and voting information and activate field work only under current state and local rules.

> **For:** A self-directed campaign volunteer; captains may use this for peer onboarding

> **Status:** Campaign proposal and volunteer guidance. Verify changing law and campaign instructions before regulated activity.

## Required foundation

> **Read first:** Complete the Universal Volunteer Core before using this playbook. Its truth, privacy, consent, spending, safety, and automation rules apply here in full.

## What success looks like

- Official sources and neutral assistance
- No handling ballots or registration data outside authorized systems
- No electioneering, obstruction, intimidation, or misinformation

> **Your 60-minute start:** Use the EAC state directory to find your state’s official registration and election pages. Share that official link with five people; do not collect forms unless a current campaign module authorizes it.

## Set up the action

1. Open the state/local election authority page and note its update date.
2. Use exact official deadlines, ID rules, polling information, and eligibility language; never rely on a social post.
3. Keep voter registration assistance separate from pressure to support any candidate.
4. If the campaign activates a program, use its training, forms, return schedule, and privacy process exactly.
5. For rides or accessibility assistance, use a lawful campaign-approved process and never condition help on a vote.
6. On Election Day, follow current buffer-zone, apparel, signage, poll-observer, photography, and contact rules.

## Words you can use

Opening script

> **Say this in your own voice:** Voting rules are state-specific. The official information for registration, deadlines, ID, and voting options is available through your state election authority; the EAC directory links to it. I can help you find the official page, but I can’t decide eligibility for you.

Follow-up script

> **Offer one next step:** Your vote is private and the choice is yours. If you want to read this campaign’s platform, the official site is galinsky2028.us.

Scripts are guardrails, not a performance. Keep required identification and legal language exact; otherwise sound like yourself.

## Run the action

1. Review the legal, safety, consent, and privacy checks before the first contact.
2. Begin with the smallest useful batch so errors are cheap to correct.
3. Ask permission and give the other person an easy exit.
4. Use only current official links, forms, lists, scripts, and unaltered materials.
5. Record only the requested result; honor opt-outs immediately.
6. Stop on any red-lane condition.
7. Debrief in three lines and choose the next action.

- [ ] Identity was clear.
- [ ] Consent was respected.
- [ ] Claims stayed within published material.
- [ ] No private list or unauthorized spending was created.
- [ ] The next volunteer can understand the handoff.

## When something goes wrong

| Situation | Response |
| --- | --- |
| A person asks if they are eligible | Point to the election authority; do not make the determination. |
| A registration form contains private data | Use only the authorized return process; do not copy, photograph, or retain it. |
| A polling-place dispute occurs | Do not intervene physically. Follow official escalation channels and protect access. |
| A rumor spreads about voting time/place | Do not repeat it; verify and share the official correction with date/time. |

## Stop conditions

> **Do not proceed:** Stop, preserve safety and evidence where appropriate, and switch to a green-lane action if any condition below applies.

- No current state/local module for field activity
- Handling a ballot, choosing for a voter, or viewing a private vote
- Operating inside a restricted electioneering zone
- Offering anything of value for registering, voting, or choosing a candidate

## Quality scorecard

| Measure | Good | Warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Every factual claim is in current campaign material or a dated primary source. | A number, quote, status, or promise cannot be verified. |
| Consent | People can decline easily; opt-outs are immediate. | Repeated contact, pressure, or hidden recording. |
| Privacy | Official forms handle personal information; local notes are anonymous. | Names, contact data, screenshots, or exports on personal devices. |
| Completion | One action, one owner, one deadline, one anonymous result. | Vague activity without a finish line or handoff. |
