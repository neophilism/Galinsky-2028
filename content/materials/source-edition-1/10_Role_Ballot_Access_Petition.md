# Ballot-Access Petition Playbook

*Protect every signature by refusing to guess at state law.*

Prepare volunteers for independent-candidate petition work and activate circulation only when a current, state-specific campaign module is published.

> **For:** A self-directed campaign volunteer; captains may use this for peer onboarding

> **Status:** Campaign proposal and volunteer guidance. Verify changing law and campaign instructions before regulated activity.

## Required foundation

> **Read first:** Complete the Universal Volunteer Core before using this playbook. Its truth, privacy, consent, spending, safety, and automation rules apply here in full.

## What success looks like

- No circulation on an unverified or copied form
- Exact compliance with circulator, signer, witnessing, custody, and deadline rules
- Clean chain of custody and respectful public contact

> **Your 60-minute start:** Find your state election authority through the EAC directory and read the campaign status. Do not circulate until the campaign publishes a dated State Activation Sheet and exact petition form.

## Set up the action

1. Confirm the module names the state, election, candidate, form revision, legal authority, circulation window, filing deadline, signature target, signer rules, circulator rules, witnessing/notary rules, correction rules, and custody process.
2. Confirm that two volunteers independently checked every field against the current state election authority.
3. Complete required training and eligibility affirmation before receiving numbered sheets.
4. Use only original authorized sheets; never prefill signer data or separate a petition from required attachments.
5. Witness every signature as state law requires and never leave a sheet unattended.
6. Return sheets by the internal deadline with the custody log; never keep copies or signer images.

## Words you can use

Opening script

> **Say this in your own voice:** Hi—I’m a trained volunteer collecting signatures so independent presidential candidate Phillip Galinsky can seek ballot access in this state. Signing supports ballot access; it does not require you to vote for him. May I explain the state eligibility rule before you decide?

Follow-up script

> **Offer one next step:** Please read the petition header yourself and complete only your own information. I cannot fill in, correct, or interpret anything beyond the campaign’s verified state instructions.

Scripts are guardrails, not a performance. Keep required identification and legal language exact; otherwise sound like yourself.

## Run the action

1. Review the legal, safety, consent, and privacy checks before the first contact.
2. Begin with the smallest useful batch so errors are cheap to correct.
3. Ask permission and give the other person an easy exit.
4. Use only current official links, forms, lists, scripts, and unaltered materials.
5. Record only the requested result; honor opt-outs immediately.
6. Stop on any red-lane condition.
7. Debrief in three lines and choose the next action.

- [ ] Identity was clear.
- [ ] Consent was respected.
- [ ] Claims stayed within published material.
- [ ] No private list or unauthorized spending was created.
- [ ] The next volunteer can understand the handoff.

## When something goes wrong

| Situation | Response |
| --- | --- |
| A field is unclear | Stop the signer before they write; consult the published state module. If it is not answered, do not collect the signature. |
| A signer makes an error | Follow only the module’s correction rule; never erase, white-out, or rewrite on your own. |
| A sheet is damaged or lost | Stop circulation, preserve what remains, and report immediately through the official chain-of-custody process. |
| An official or property manager directs you to move | Comply calmly and document the location/rule later. |

## Stop conditions

> **Do not proceed:** Stop, preserve safety and evidence where appropriate, and switch to a green-lane action if any condition below applies.

- No dated State Activation Sheet
- Any mismatch in candidate name, election, form revision, date, or attachment
- Unclear circulator eligibility, residency, witness, notary, custody, or deadline rule
- Payment per signature, forged/prefilled data, signer coercion, or unsupervised sheet custody

## Quality scorecard

| Measure | Good | Warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Every factual claim is in current campaign material or a dated primary source. | A number, quote, status, or promise cannot be verified. |
| Consent | People can decline easily; opt-outs are immediate. | Repeated contact, pressure, or hidden recording. |
| Privacy | Official forms handle personal information; local notes are anonymous. | Names, contact data, screenshots, or exports on personal devices. |
| Completion | One action, one owner, one deadline, one anonymous result. | Vague activity without a finish line or handoff. |
