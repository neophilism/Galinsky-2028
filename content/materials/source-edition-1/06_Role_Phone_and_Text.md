# Phone & Text Volunteer Playbook

*Make person-to-person contacts while protecting consent and campaign data.*

Run lawful manual phone or text outreach, including volunteer call-bank gatherings, through approved campaign systems.

> **For:** A self-directed campaign volunteer; captains may use this for peer onboarding

> **Status:** Campaign proposal and volunteer guidance. Verify changing law and campaign instructions before regulated activity.

## Required foundation

> **Read first:** Complete the Universal Volunteer Core before using this playbook. Its truth, privacy, consent, spending, safety, and automation rules apply here in full.

## What success looks like

- One person initiates one contact at a time
- Immediate opt-out handling
- No automated, prerecorded, artificial-voice, or scraped-list activity

> **Your 60-minute start:** If an official campaign list and script are active, make five manual calls. Otherwise call two people you personally know and invite them to read one policy.

## Set up the action

1. Confirm that the list, platform, script, calling hours, and state instructions are current.
2. Use headphones and a private setting; no speakerphone where others can hear personal information.
3. At a call-bank party, designate a sober host, review opt-outs, and keep screens out of photographs.
4. Read the required identification exactly; speak naturally after it.
5. Record only the outcomes offered by the official tool.
6. Log out, clear local downloads, and report any data exposure.

## Words you can use

Opening script

> **Say this in your own voice:** Hi, may I speak with [name]? My name is [first name], and I’m a volunteer with Phillip Galinsky for America. We’re sharing the independent 2028 platform. Is now an okay time for a brief question?

Follow-up script

> **Offer one next step:** Thank you. You can read the platform and choose whether to volunteer or donate at galinsky2028.us. Would you like me to end the call here?

Scripts are guardrails, not a performance. Keep required identification and legal language exact; otherwise sound like yourself.

## Run the action

1. Review the legal, safety, consent, and privacy checks before the first contact.
2. Begin with the smallest useful batch so errors are cheap to correct.
3. Ask permission and give the other person an easy exit.
4. Use only current official links, forms, lists, scripts, and unaltered materials.
5. Record only the requested result; honor opt-outs immediately.
6. Stop on any red-lane condition.
7. Debrief in three lines and choose the next action.

- [ ] Identity was clear.
- [ ] Consent was respected.
- [ ] Claims stayed within published material.
- [ ] No private list or unauthorized spending was created.
- [ ] The next volunteer can understand the handoff.

## When something goes wrong

| Situation | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong number | Apologize, mark the supplied outcome, and do not call back. |
| Stop request | Apologize once, mark do-not-contact immediately, and end. |
| Voicemail | Use only the current approved voicemail; never use prerecorded drops. |
| No official system is active | Contact only people you personally know; do not improvise a list. |

## Stop conditions

> **Do not proceed:** Stop, preserve safety and evidence where appropriate, and switch to a green-lane action if any condition below applies.

- Autodialing, prerecorded or artificial voice, ringless voicemail, or bulk text
- Purchased, scraped, shared, or personally exported campaign lists
- Calling outside current campaign/state hours
- Continuing after an opt-out or debating an angry recipient

## Quality scorecard

| Measure | Good | Warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Every factual claim is in current campaign material or a dated primary source. | A number, quote, status, or promise cannot be verified. |
| Consent | People can decline easily; opt-outs are immediate. | Repeated contact, pressure, or hidden recording. |
| Privacy | Official forms handle personal information; local notes are anonymous. | Names, contact data, screenshots, or exports on personal devices. |
| Completion | One action, one owner, one deadline, one anonymous result. | Vague activity without a finish line or handoff. |
