# Volunteer Forms & Templates Pack

*Self-service worksheets for planning, execution, verification, and clean handoffs.*

Provide printable and copyable tools that reduce ambiguity without creating local supporter databases or substituting for official campaign systems.

> **For:** All volunteers; captains and facilitators may reproduce individual pages without altering required warnings

> **Status:** Campaign proposal and volunteer guidance. Verify changing law and campaign instructions before regulated activity.

## How to use these forms

> **Privacy rule:** These forms are for plans, anonymous totals, verification, and handoffs. Do not write supporter names, contact details, political profiles, payment data, voter-file data, medical information, immigration information, ancestry documents, or confidential campaign information on them.

- Complete only the form needed for the action.
- Submit through the current official campaign channel if one exists.
- Keep no duplicate after a successful handoff unless the form explicitly requires custody retention.
- Blank lines are intentional fillable areas, not missing campaign instructions.
- A worksheet never activates a red-lane tactic.

## Incident First-Response Card

> **Immediate danger:** Call 911 or the appropriate local emergency number first. Move to safety. Do not investigate, confront, or post publicly.

1. Protect people and end the activity.
2. Preserve only necessary evidence without spreading private information.
3. Secure campaign data/account access if exposed.
4. Write objective facts: who/what/when/where, direct words if remembered, witnesses, actions taken, and whether emergency services were contacted.
5. Submit through the current official incident channel.
6. Do not promise confidentiality, legal outcomes, reimbursement, discipline, or public response.
