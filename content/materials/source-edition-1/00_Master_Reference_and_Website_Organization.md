# Master Reference & Website Organization

*The owner’s map for a self-directed national volunteer system.*

Map every deliverable, show how volunteers move through the system, recommend a simple single-page website structure, and define how the packet set stays current.

> **For:** Campaign owner, volunteer captains, website editor, compliance leads, and future document maintainers

> **Status:** Campaign proposal and volunteer guidance. Verify changing law and campaign instructions before regulated activity.

## The system at a glance

> **Design principle:** Centralize truth; decentralize lawful action. One official platform and one universal rule set feed many self-service volunteer roles. Regulated actions activate only through current campaign/state modules.

| Layer | Documents | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Master Reference & Website Organization | Owner’s map: what exists, how documents relate, publication order, maintenance. |
| Start | Universal Volunteer Core | Required operating rules linked before every role. |
| Act | 12 Role Playbooks | Step-by-step instructions, scripts, safeguards, troubleshooting, completion standards. |
| Learn | 17 Policy Action Kits | Accurate explanations, limits, questions, actions, and primary sources. |
| Explain | Campaign & Pillar Messaging Guide | Message architecture across the whole platform. |
| Teach | Orientation Facilitator Guide + slide deck | A ready-to-run live or self-directed orientation. |
| Operate | Forms & Templates Pack | Printable/replicable worksheets and verification tools. |
| Publish | Website Copy & Onboarding Messages | Page copy, tabs, calls to action, and follow-up sequence. |
| Archive | Complete Volunteer Manual | One compiled reference containing the operating system, roles, policy kits, and tools. |

Every public role begins with the Universal Volunteer Core. A role playbook then explains the action. A policy action kit supplies the message. Forms provide execution discipline. The complete manual is a reference copy, not the preferred entry point for a new volunteer.

## How the documents relate

| Volunteer need | Open first | Then open | Use to finish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I have 15–60 minutes | Universal Core: green lane | Personal Network or Digital Ambassador | Official site link + anonymous activity report |
| I want to explain an idea | Universal Core: truth rules | Issue Ambassador | One matching Policy Action Kit |
| I want to meet voters | Universal Core: safety/consent | Canvassing, Phone/Text, or Event Host | Current official tool/script + event/action forms |
| I want to lead | Universal Core | Recruiter or Local Captain | Orientation Guide + weekly planning/debrief forms |
| I want ballot/election work | Universal Core: red lane | Ballot Access or Voter/GOTV | Dated State Activation Sheet; do nothing regulated without it |
| I have a professional skill | Universal Core: privacy/IP | Skills & Operations | Creative/research QA and handoff forms |

> **The activation gate:** Ballot-access petitioning, state-regulated registration/GOTV, paid communications, mass calling/texting, spending, contracts, private-data access, and official-account publishing are never activated by a general packet. They require a dated campaign tool or module for the exact jurisdiction and activity.

## Recommended single-page website structure

Use one volunteer action page with a short fixed header and five primary tabs or anchored sections. Five tabs are easier to scan than twelve role tabs; place role cards inside the relevant tab. Keep the Universal Core and safety rules visible from every tab.

| Tab | Contents | Primary call to action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Start Here | Welcome, 60-second campaign summary, green/yellow/red lanes, required Universal Core, three-question role chooser. | Choose my first action |
| 2. Choose a Role | Cards for the 12 roles grouped as Share, Reach, Gather, Lead, Protect the Vote, and Build. | Open a role playbook |
| 3. Learn an Issue | Five pillar filters leading to 17 policy action kits; each card shows one sentence, guardrail, and share link. | Open an action kit |
| 4. Host or Lead | Event Host, Recruiter, Local Captain, orientation deck/guide, forms, call-bank party instructions. | Plan a lawful event |
| 5. Safety & Rules | Universal legal/privacy/safety rules, active state notices, opt-out rules, incident reporting, document version/date. | Check whether my action is active |

- Keep a persistent ‘Donate through the official form’ button separate from volunteer instructions; never ask a volunteer to process money.
- Show ‘proposal, not current law’ on every policy card and ‘state module required’ on petition/election cards.
- Offer HTML first for accessibility and search; offer DOCX/PDF downloads as secondary options.
- Use filters and expandable sections rather than sending newcomers to a large ZIP or the complete manual.
- Show edition date and last legal review date on every page. Archive superseded versions away from search and remove their public links.

## Role chooser

Ask only three questions: How much time do you have now? Do you want to talk with people or work behind the scenes? Are you willing to complete training for a regulated activity? Route by answers, not by demographics.

| Preference | Best first role | Next role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15–30 minutes; private | Personal Network Organizer | Digital Ambassador |
| 30–60 minutes; online | Digital Ambassador | Issue Ambassador |
| 1–2 hours; public contact | Canvasser & Tabling | Phone & Text |
| 2–4 hours; bring people together | Event Host | Recruiter & Onboarder |
| Weekly coordination | Local Team Captain | Orientation Facilitator |
| Legal-detail work | Ballot Access or Voter/GOTV | State compliance lead after activation |
| Professional/practical skill | Skills & Operations | Policy research or accessibility review |

> **Default route:** If no answer fits, assign Personal Network Organizer. It is lawful, low-cost, relationship-based, and immediately useful when performed one person at a time.

## Twelve roles

| # | Role | First action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Personal Network Organizer | Make a private list of ten people you genuinely know, choose one issue relevant to each, and send five personal messages. |
| 2 | Digital Ambassador | Choose one official policy page, write a three-sentence personal reaction, link the source, and invite one sincere question. |
| 3 | Issue Ambassador | Pick one policy kit, practice its 30-second explanation aloud three times, and have one permission-based conversation. |
| 4 | Canvassing & Tabling | Choose a lawful public location, recruit a buddy, confirm any venue rules, and practice a fifteen-second opener before going out. |
| 5 | Phone & Text Volunteer | If an official campaign list and script are active, make five manual calls. Otherwise call two people you personally know and invite them to read one policy. |
| 6 | Event Host | Invite four people to a forty-five-minute discussion of one policy, using your home or a lawful no-cost space. |
| 7 | Volunteer Recruiter & Onboarder | Ask one interested person three questions—time, preferred mode, and comfort with public contact—then point them to one role packet and one first action. |
| 8 | Local Team Captain | Recruit one co-captain, choose one green-lane weekly action, and schedule a twenty-minute check-in using the standard agenda. |
| 9 | Ballot-Access Petition | Find your state election authority through the EAC directory and read the campaign status. Do not circulate until the campaign publishes a dated State Activation Sheet and exact petition form. |
| 10 | Voter Registration, GOTV & Election-Day | Use the EAC state directory to find your state’s official registration and election pages. Share that official link with five people; do not collect forms unless a current campaign module authorizes it. |
| 11 | Small-Dollar Fundraising | Send three personal messages explaining why you volunteer and linking to the official donation form reached from galinsky2028.us. |
| 12 | Skills & Operations Volunteer | Choose one public-source task, write a five-line brief, create a small draft, and submit it through the current official volunteer channel without publishing it as campaign-official. |

## Publication order

1. Publish the Universal Volunteer Core and the Safety & Rules tab.
2. Publish the role chooser and the three green-lane roles: Personal Network, Digital Ambassador, and Issue Ambassador.
3. Publish the remaining nonregulated role playbooks and forms.
4. Publish the policy action kits with the current official platform text and primary-source links.
5. Publish Host/Lead materials and orientation deck.
6. Publish ballot-access and voter/GOTV packets in clearly inactive status until an exact state module is verified.
7. Activate each state or tool with a dated notice; never silently convert an inactive packet into live instructions.
8. Publish the complete manual and archive only after all individual pages are live.

> **Nothing is live by implication:** A PDF existing on the site does not activate a tactic. Put an explicit ACTIVE / NOT ACTIVE status beside regulated actions.

## Governance and maintenance

| Cadence | Check | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before each use | Official platform text, live form, tool, script, disclaimer, activity status | Use current version only |
| Monthly | Broken links, accessibility, forms, opt-outs, permissions, stale event copy | Repair or unpublish |
| Quarterly | FEC/FCC/EAC guidance, campaign finance process, privacy/security controls | Legal/compliance review date |
| Before state activation | Statute, regulations, official forms, deadlines, circulator/signer rules, buffer zones | Two-person verification and dated module |
| After platform revision | Every summary, script, FAQ, slide, and social sample | Update atomically; archive old edition |

- Use semantic versioning for public materials: major for rule/position changes, minor for substantive guidance, patch for typo/link corrections.
- Maintain a source register with title, issuing body, URL, publication/update date, retrieval date, claim supported, and reviewer initials.
- Keep a change log visible to captains. For material corrections, publish what changed and instruct volunteers to discard prior copies.
- Never leave a known unsafe or legally stale document online while waiting for a perfect replacement; unpublish it.

## Owner launch checklist

- [ ] Universal Core is public and linked before all roles.
- [ ] Every policy page says proposal, not current law.
- [ ] Every regulated role displays ACTIVE or NOT ACTIVE.
- [ ] All links point to the official domain or named primary authorities.
- [ ] Official forms—not volunteer spreadsheets—receive personal data.
- [ ] Donation flow is separate and volunteers never take custody.
- [ ] Mobile, keyboard, contrast, alt-text, captions, and screen-reader checks passed.
- [ ] A correction/unpublish mechanism exists.
- [ ] Edition date and disclaimer appear on every download.
- [ ] A second person can follow the site without verbal instructions.
