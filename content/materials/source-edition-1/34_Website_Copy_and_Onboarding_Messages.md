## Start Here tab

> **Heading:** Act independently. Never guess at law, safety, consent, money, or data.

Most volunteer work is simple: read the official material, speak truthfully, respect a no, and direct people back to the campaign site. Some work needs an active campaign system or current state instructions. Use the three lanes below before you begin.

| Card | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| GREEN — ACT NOW | Share official public links, talk with people you know, host a small no-cost discussion, learn a policy, or submit a draft based on public sources. Do not collect private data or spend money. |
| YELLOW — USE THE CAMPAIGN SYSTEM | Canvass, call, text, table, host larger events, recruit, or access tools only through the current official script, form, list, training, and permissions. |
| RED — STOP AND VERIFY | Petitions, voter registration, paid reach, mass calls/texts, contracts, fundraising custody, permits, data exports, and official publishing require a dated activation or written authorization. |

> **Required checkbox:** I have read the Universal Volunteer Core and understand that a missing instruction does not authorize a regulated tactic.

## Three-question role chooser

> **Intro:** Answer three questions. We do not ask for demographic or sensitive information.

1. How much time do you have: 15–30 minutes, 1–2 hours, 2–4 hours, or weekly?
2. Do you prefer private conversations, public contact, online work, hosting, leadership, election-detail work, or behind-the-scenes skill work?
3. Are you willing to wait for current training before any regulated activity?

> **Default result:** Start with Personal Network Organizer: send five personal, permission-based messages about one campaign idea.

## Host or Lead tab

> **Heading:** Build a circle that can act without waiting for staff.

Start small: a four-person policy conversation, a volunteer orientation, a no-cost planning session, or a call-bank party where real people manually call through the active campaign system. Use the Event Host or Local Team Captain playbook, the orientation deck, and the printable forms.

> **Call-bank notice:** A social call-bank party is welcome when humans place one call at a time using a current approved list, tool, script, hours, and opt-out process. Automated dialing, prerecorded/artificial voice, ringless voicemail, bulk text, scraped lists, and screen photos are not allowed.

## Safety & Rules tab

> **Heading:** Protect the person, the vote, the data, and the truth.

- Respect ‘no’ immediately—at a door, on a call, in a message, or at an event.
- Use official forms for signups and donations. Never create a private supporter database.
- Use your own time and device. Assume spending and reimbursement are not authorized.
- Never threaten, harass, dox, obstruct, trespass, carry a weapon while volunteering, or work impaired.
- For immediate danger, call emergency services.
- For petitions and election work, the card must say ACTIVE for the exact state, election, and activity.

> **Status component:** ACTIVITY STATUS — State: [selected by visitor] • Activity: [selected by visitor] • ACTIVE / NOT ACTIVE • Reviewed: [published campaign date] • Open the dated State Activation Sheet

The bracketed selections above describe interface-controlled values populated by the website, not text a volunteer should type or guess.

## Self-service onboarding sequence

> **Message 1 — immediately:** Subject: Your first campaign action takes 60 minutes or less

Thanks for stepping forward. Start with the Universal Volunteer Core, then open the one role packet you selected. Choose a quantity, a finish time, and a stop condition. If your role depends on an official tool or state activation that is not live, complete the packet’s green-lane alternative today.

> **Message 2 — after two days:** Subject: Finish one small action

A completed action beats a vague commitment. Send five personal messages, share one sourced policy, practice one explanation, invite four people, or submit one bounded skill draft. Use the official form for every signup and the three-line debrief when you finish.

> **Message 3 — after seven days:** Subject: Repeat, deepen, or bring one person

Choose one: repeat the same action with better technique, learn the matching policy kit, or onboard one person into a role. Do not expand into money, data, automation, ballot, election, contract, or permit work unless the campaign has activated the exact process.

> **Opt-out footer:** You are receiving volunteer information because you submitted the campaign’s official form. Use the current unsubscribe method in this message to stop future contact.
